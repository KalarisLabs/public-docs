**Kuchi measures and plans; your agent sees and builds.** The workflow has five steps. Each tool result tells the
agent what to do next.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a reference">
    The agent calls `kuchi_suggest_templates` and shows you each candidate's poster and link. You pick one in chat, or
    pick a video on [kuchi.kalarislabs.com](https://kuchi.kalarislabs.com/) and paste its link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Study it">
    The agent calls `kuchi_get_breakdown`, looks at the storyboard sheets, and classifies what each shot shows against
    the 14 shot kinds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan the remake">
    The agent calls `kuchi_plan_remake` with your product details and its shot analysis. Kuchi returns a blueprint that
    re-times every reference shot to your target length on whole frames, and lists the assets it still needs, such as
    your logo and screenshots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build">
    The agent builds to the blueprint's frames, using only your product, assets and copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check before delivery">
    The agent calls `kuchi_check_remake` with its shot list. Kuchi flags reused brand words, borrowed assets, invented
    logos or UI and timing gaps, and returns matching frame times to compare.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the frame-by-frame breakdown works

A script decodes every frame of every video and records where each shot starts and ends, how it enters, how much of
the frame moves, its colors and its loudness. The same file always gives the same result, and a test checks that each
video's shots cover every frame exactly once. Shot classes such as entrance, motion level and tone come from fixed
thresholds on those measurements, so they are reproducible rather than a model's opinion.

What a shot *shows*, such as a logo, a product screen or a chart, is a judgement, so Kuchi does not guess it. The
connected agent reads it from the storyboard sheets. A curator can review a video and write labels, which then take
the place of the agent's reading for that shot. Labels stop being served if the video is re-measured and its shots
change.

## Rules every remake follows

- Copy the reference's motion and taste: timing, transitions, layout and color balance.
- Take everything on screen from your product.
- Never reuse the reference's brand, footage or music.
