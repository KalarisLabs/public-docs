**Kuchi is a library of real product-launch videos that your agent uses as inspiration templates.** Each video is
measured frame by frame. Your agent picks a reference, studies its shots, plans a remake that uses your product,
assets and copy, and checks the result against the reference. It copies motion and taste, never the reference's brand,
footage or music.

```text Setup prompt
Connect the Kuchi MCP server at https://kuchi.kalarislabs.com/mcp (Streamable HTTP, no sign-in) and confirm it works by listing its tools.
```

## What is in the library

| Style category | Videos |
|---|---|
| Device and UI transformations: phones, floating windows, camera moves, interfaces that change shape | 19 |
| Clean UI, data and graphics | 20 |
| Pastel UI and cursor demos | 12 |
| Typography and abstract motion | 11 |
| Dark, gradient and mixed media | 19 |
| Illustrated mascot stories | 3 |
| **Total** | **84** |

Every entry includes a playable MP4, a poster, a remake prompt, historical post stats and credit to the original
creator. Kuchi's data files are in the [repository](https://github.com/KalarisLabs/kuchi/tree/main/data).

## What Kuchi does and what your agent does

| Kuchi supplies | Your agent does |
|---|---|
| Exact shot boundaries, entrances, motion levels, colors and loudness, measured from every frame | Reads storyboard frames and decides what each shot shows |
| Storyboard sheets of real frames | Builds the video with your product, assets and copy |
| A blueprint that re-times every reference shot to your target length on whole frames | Asks you for missing assets |
| Checks for reused brand words, borrowed assets and timing drift | Compares its frames with the reference before delivering |

Without MCP, the same data is available as JSON at `https://kuchi.kalarislabs.com/agent/catalog.json`.
