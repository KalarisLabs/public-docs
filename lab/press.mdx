## Boilerplate

Use this text as is, or shorten it without changing its meaning.

```text boilerplate.txt
Kalaris Labs is a research lab building the infrastructure for the agentic era. Its work spans agent
harnesses, continual and reinforcement learning, multi-agent simulation, game theory and the economics of
agents, agent protocols, evaluations, and AI safety and security.
```

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|

| Base | India |
| Field | AI research |

| Principle | AI for good |
| Founded | 2026 |
| Founder | [Sayan Chowdhury](/lab/sayan-chowdhury) |
| Website | [kalarislabs.com](https://kalarislabs.com) |
| Motto | "The rules have to exist before the actors do." |

## What we are working toward

- Infrastructure for the agentic era, built in the open.
- Open protocols for how agents communicate and transact.
- Multi-agent simulation and game theory for the agentic economy.
- Evaluations that are hard to game, with safety research throughout.
