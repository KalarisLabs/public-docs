**Sayan Chowdhury is the founder of [Kalaris Labs](/lab/about) and an agentic AI researcher based in India.** The work
covers agent systems architecture, scientific infrastructure and the economic layer of agentic systems: the
harnesses, protocols, markets and evaluations that agents need before they can be trusted with real work.

Sayan Chowdhury is also the author of the lab's preprints and writes most of the [Kalaris Labs blog](/blog).
