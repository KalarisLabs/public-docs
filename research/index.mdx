**Kalaris Labs has published 17 research papers since October 2026, all open access.** Sixteen are on Zenodo with a
DOI and are listed on [Sayan Chowdhury's ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0004-8421-0087). The fly brain study is
published with its code on GitHub. Read the **Evidence** column before you cite a result: several papers are proposals
that report no experiments.

| Evidence level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <Badge color="green" icon="flask-conical">Experiment</Badge> | Reports measured results from experiments |
| <Badge color="blue" icon="cpu">Simulation</Badge> | Results come from a simulator, not from deployed systems |
| <Badge color="yellow" icon="wrench">Partial implementation</Badge> | Some components run; reports no benchmark measurements |
| <Badge color="gray" icon="pencil-ruler">Proposal</Badge> | Design or pre-registration; reports no experimental results |
| <Badge color="purple" icon="message-square-quote">Position</Badge> | Argument; reports no experiments |

Each paper page has a BibTeX entry. Cite the DOI, not this page, so that citations count toward the paper. Zenodo
gives each paper a concept DOI that always resolves to the latest version.
